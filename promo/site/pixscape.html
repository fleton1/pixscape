An old school adventure

What's in it

A whole world, drawn in code

Every sprite, tile and tune is generated by the game at start-up. The game loads no image files at all.

Towns to come home to

Brindlewood, Highcrest, Port Selby, Vesperhold and more, with banks, shops, furnaces and people going about their day.

560 by 440 tiles

Forests, desert, swamp, frozen peaks, volcanic islands and the Vesperan Steppe, linked by ships, spells and rings of standing stones.

Skilling at 1x

Mining, woodcutting, fishing, smithing, cooking and the rest, on the classic XP curve. Every level unlocks something.

Farming in real time

Allotments, herbs and trees keep growing while the game is closed. Rake, plant, compost, come back later.

Bosses with mechanics

Emberwing rains fire you have to step out of. The Bone Tyrant slams. The Scarab King summons swarms. Most of them drop a pet.

35 spells

Strike to Wave for every element, autocast from a staff, teleports, alchemy, superheat and enchanted jewellery.

A real combat triangle

Dragons are weak to arrows, ghosts shrug off swords, and chinchompas hit everything standing next to the target.

The Wilderness

Aggressive monsters and a level counter. Die out here and you keep only your three most valuable items.

Dungeons with their own light

Ten dungeons, from the Brindlewood Sewers to the Abyssal Rift, each with its own lighting and music.

The Frost Heart

Chop frozen roots, keep four braziers lit and fed, and wear down the heart before the cold wears you down.

World events

Every 15 to 30 minutes something happens: a shooting star to mine, a goblin raid, a merchant with rare stock.

The Vesper Exchange

Trade almost anything at market prices that drift over the day and move with your own buying and selling.

Skills

22 skills, 13,034,431 XP each to max

The same XP curve as the original, at 1x by default. Level 92 is still halfway to 99.

  • Slayer tasks from three masters, with slayer-only monsters.
  • Herblore from 14 herbs into 18 potions.
  • Agility courses with marks of grace, plus shortcuts.
  • Runecraft at 12 altars hidden behind ruins.
  • Tap any skill in game for its full unlock guide.
Play anywhere

Browser or Android, portrait or landscape

Tap to walk, press and hold for options, pinch to zoom. Your save exports to a file and moves between devices.
